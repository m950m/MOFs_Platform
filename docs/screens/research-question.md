# Research question

Where your investigation starts: record **what you are looking for**, and the
tool keeps it visible everywhere it matters.

## What enters

- **Question wording** — your research question, in your words.
- **Reaction / application** — e.g. HER, OER, or both (kept independent).
- **Allowed material classes** — e.g. MOF, ZIF, composite, derived.
- **Relevant conditions** — electrolyte, pH, temperature, …
- **Hard requirements** — must be met by a candidate.
- **Preferences** — nice to have, not required. The two are visibly separate.
- **Meaning of improvement** — what "better" means *for this question*; the
  tool never fixes a universal target.

Saving is explicit: **Save question**. Unsaved edits are discarded when you
navigate away — only an explicit save persists.

## What comes out

- The saved question with its last-updated time and the number of save events
  (first save + corrections).
- **Consistency hints** — review suggestions about the wording. Nothing is
  changed by them.
- **Mechanical observations** — labeled `tool inference`: vague terms from a
  documented list, empty refinement fields. Review suggestions only.
- **Optional AI refinement** (Z.ai GLM): enable per session,
  paste a key (session-only, never stored), request suggestions. Only the
  question's own saved fields are sent anywhere. Applying a suggestion
  prefills the form — *you* still press Save. Failure kinds are typed and
  honest (no_key / bad_input / timeout / offline / rate_limited /
  bad_response), each with a next step.

## Honest markers

- Every field you leave blank displays as **`unknown`** — it is never
  defaulted or guessed.
- The save event itself establishes nothing scientific: *saving a question
  establishes no material identity, measured performance, laboratory
  feasibility, novelty, or scientific conclusion.*
